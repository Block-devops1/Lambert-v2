Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-29. Effective 29 October 2026.

Lambert is a habit tracker with an AI coach. This policy explains what personal data Lambert collects, why, who else handles it, and your rights. It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Who we are

Lambert is run by [[OWNER NAME]] (“we”), based in Nigeria. We decide how your data is used, which makes us the data controller. Contact us at [[CONTACT EMAIL]].

2. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your password (stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider), your account status, when you signed up, and how many coach messages you have sent today.
  • What you log: activity names, minutes, whether each is a build habit or a disruptor, optional notes, and the reasons you give for missed habits.
  • Goals and settings: your goals, weekly challenge, facts you ask Lambert to remember, your daily goal and weekly habit targets.
  • Chats: your messages to Lambert, his replies, and which AI model wrote each reply.
  • Technical data: your IP address and request details in our hosting logs. Your login session is kept in your browser’s local storage on your own device. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Be aware: habit logs and chats can reveal sensitive things such as sleep, health, mood or personal circumstances. Only enter what you are comfortable sharing. By using Lambert you consent to us processing whatever you choose to enter.

3. How we use it

To run the app, calculate your streaks and stats, generate coach replies, keep the service secure, enforce approval and usage limits, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

4. How the AI coach uses your data

When you message Lambert, our server sends our AI provider, Groq, your message, your recent conversation, your goals, saved memories and weekly challenge, and a summary of your habit data (habit names, minutes, streaks and the reasons you gave for missed habits). It never includes other users’ data.

Groq’s published data policy says it does not retain customer data for AI requests by default, but it may temporarily log inputs and outputs, for up to 30 days, to troubleshoot reliability or investigate abuse unless Zero Data Retention is switched on. It says any data it does retain is held in the United States, and that it does not use customer data to train models for its own purposes. Read it at console.groq.com/docs/your-data. We do not train AI models on your content either.

AI replies can be wrong. Lambert is not a doctor, therapist or emergency service. See our Terms.

5. Who else handles your data

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, and the emails it sends you (such as confirmation). Data region: [[SUPABASE REGION]].
  • Vercel: hosting and server logs.
  • Groq: generates coach replies, as described above.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and a code library when a page opens, which shows them your IP address.

These providers only process data to run Lambert. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

6. Data processed outside Nigeria

Your data is processed outside Nigeria, including in the United States by Groq. By accepting this policy when you sign up, you consent to these transfers. You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete your account.

7. Who can see your data

You can. We can too: as the administrator we can access stored data, including chats. We only look at your content when needed to fix a problem, investigate abuse, or follow the law. Lambert is not end-to-end encrypted. Data is encrypted in transit over HTTPS, and access rules stop other users from reading your records through the app.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Ask us to delete your account and data and we will do so within 30 days. Copies may remain briefly in provider backups, and Groq’s logs follow Groq’s own schedule above.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to give you a copy of your data (including in a portable format), correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw consent. Email [[CONTACT EMAIL]] and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

10. Security and breaches

We use reasonable measures to protect your data. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware and tell affected users without undue delay where the risk is high.

11. Age

Lambert is for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the version and date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant. Questions: [[CONTACT EMAIL]].
